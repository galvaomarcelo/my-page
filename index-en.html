01 — About

Software engineering with a geospatial mindset.

I hold a degree in Computer Science from the University of Brasília, an MSc in Geospatial Technologies (Erasmus Mundus), and a Ph.D. in Geoinformatics from the University of Münster, Germany. My specialties are software engineering, geospatial systems, digital cartography, and mobile applications — fields I've been passionate about since my undergraduate years and have been exploring through research and the development of practical solutions.

Throughout my career, I've worked on major technology projects such as the United Nations Renewable Energy Observatory, WAYTO — a research project focused on developing navigation systems — and GeoAR — high-precision, geographically aware augmented reality. Currently, I develop software solutions for the agricultural sector, integrating spatial data, artificial intelligence, computer vision, and mobile applications.

I have experience with a wide range of technical tools. I work with Java, Python, C#, JavaScript, Kotlin (Android), APIs, spatial databases (PostGIS), web and mobile applications, data science, and Unity 3D. Always integrating software engineering, spatial data, and intelligent methods to bring ideas to life and build everything from research prototypes to applications used in real operations.

Cattle-Vision

Ongoing work

Cattle-Vision is a Computer Vision / GeoAI system for detecting and classifying cattle in drone imagery captured over a rural cattle farm.

The system uses aerial imagery, geospatial information, and Deep Learning to support automated inventory and monitoring of cattle and pastures.

With this system, farmers can know the exact location of their animals within hours. And answer questions like "How many calves were born in that lot?" or "What is the animal-to-area ratio in that pasture?"

GeoAR

Research Project · TU Vienna

GeoAR is a geographically aware augmented reality application, developed during my postdoc at TU Vienna.

Built primarily for the Microsoft HoloLens 2, GeoAR is one of the available AR applications with the highest positioning accuracy in outdoor urban environments — capable of highlighting objects and buildings accurately even at distances of over 1 km.

GeoAR is available for free with a tutorial to start from scratch and demonstrating all features.

03 — Professional Experience

Research experience, engineering mindset.

2022 — 2024
Geoinformation Researcher
TU Vienna · Department of Geodesy and Geoinformation

During my postdoc in Vienna, I led the Mixed-Reality Lab, focusing on Augmented Reality (AR) for outdoor environments, aiming to improve visualization accuracy and application usability. In this context, I designed and developed the GeoAR framework, integrating a C# (.NET) AR application, a Python API, a PostgreSQL database, an Android app, and the MQTT protocol. Beyond research and development, I taught courses in Computational Geometry, Software Engineering, GIS Implementation, and Mobile GIS.

2016 — 2021
Geoinformatics Researcher
University of Münster · Institute for Geoinformatics

I worked within the WayTO project, funded by the European Union (Horizon 2020), which sought to innovate the concept of navigation systems. I developed systems encompassing cartographic generalization, computational geometry, optimization, and map usability. I also built research tools such as 3D city models and driving simulators to test navigation system prototypes. In addition, I was responsible for teaching programming and digital cartography courses. Following the research work, I earned a Dr. rer. nat. (Ph.D.) in Geoinformatics from the University of Münster, Germany, with the thesis "Schematic Maps for Navigation Systems".

2012 — 2014
IT & Communication Technician
United Nations Industrial Development Organization

At UNIDO, I worked as an ICT Technician at the Renewable Energy Observatory for Latin America and the Caribbean, developing and maintaining geoinformation systems for renewable energy projects. I also supported the development of a document management system (Alfresco) and was responsible for administering web servers on Red Hat Linux, as well as internal newsletters and product websites and portals.
